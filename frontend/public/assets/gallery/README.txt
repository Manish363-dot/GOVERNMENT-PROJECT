Hello!

Please place all your 15-20 gallery photos inside this folder.
For the gallery slider to pick them up automatically, you have two options:

OPTION A (Easiest Method):
Rename your photos as:
1.jpg
2.jpg
3.jpg
... and so on up to 15.jpg or 20.jpg.

OPTION B:
If you want to keep their original names (e.g. "my-photo.png"), you can open `frontend/src/components/BlogGalleryModal.tsx` and edit the `GALLERY_IMAGES` array with your actual file names.
